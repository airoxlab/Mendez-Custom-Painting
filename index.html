Coastal conditions

Paint on the Peninsula has a harder job to do.

Marine air, morning fog that sits until noon, and afternoon sun coming off the bay are hard on an exterior. Coatings that are fine inland chalk, blister, and let go early out here.

That means prep that accounts for moisture, primers chosen for the substrate, and finish coats specified for coastal exposure. It costs a little more time up front and buys years on the back end.

  • Surfaces washed and fully dry before coating
  • Failing paint scraped back, not painted over
  • Caulking and patching at every joint that lets water in
  • Coatings chosen for salt air and UV, not just color
